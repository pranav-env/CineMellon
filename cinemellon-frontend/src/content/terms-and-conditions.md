## 2. About CineMellon®

CineMellon® is an AI-powered movie exploration app built by **Team Arsonists**, a two-person student team, as an educational project — currently developed and shared as part of an internship. It is a demo/portfolio product, not a commercial service.

## 3. Description of the Service

CineMellon® lets you:

- **Search** for movies and TV series
- View a **Movie Details** page with facts (title, cast, crew, genres, runtime, poster) and AI-generated commentary — trivia, a production timeline, hidden details, easter eggs, character analysis, cinematography and sound/music breakdowns, iconic quotes, awards & reception, cultural legacy, a separately-gated Ending Explained section, and similar-movie suggestions
- View a **Series Details** page with core facts and cast, sourced from TVmaze
- Browse **Trending Discussions** — a curated feed of discussion-style cards referencing outside film publications
- Save titles to a personal **Favourites** list, stored only in your own browser (see the [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for details) — no account needed

Movie facts for a small set of hand-picked films are built directly into the app; everything else is looked up from TMDB, with OMDB as an automatic fallback. Series data comes from TVmaze. The deeper AI-generated commentary sections are currently generated for movies only — series pages show core details and cast. Trending Discussions is fixed, curated content rather than a live feed.

## 4. AI-Generated Content Disclaimer

**Please read this section carefully — it's the most important one.**

The trivia, timelines, hidden details, easter eggs, quotes, and "similar movies" suggestions shown on this Site are generated by a third-party large language model (Anthropic's Claude and/or Google's Gemini, depending on configuration). **This content is provided for entertainment and illustrative purposes only.** Don't treat it as a verified, authoritative, or fact-checked source.
